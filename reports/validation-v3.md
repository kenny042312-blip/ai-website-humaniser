## Scenario-based instruction review

This is a direct review of how the written instructions address these requests. It is not an independent model run or a set of generated websites.

| Request | Required behavior checked in the instructions |
|---|---|
| “Build a landing page for this real product; no prices or reviews are supplied.” | Claim ledger keeps those facts unknown; demonstration can supply evidence; no invented testimonials, guarantees or numeric gains |
| “Only tighten the heading spacing; keep Inter and our purple rounded cards.” | Narrow routing stays local; optional recipe does not override brand choices or trigger a new funnel |
| “Humanise this dense logistics dashboard.” | Content-form and operational-task guidance remain primary; landing-page structure and animated tagline do not become mandatory |
| “Use the Elaya style for one concept.” | All source visual tables/options are available; deviations are identified; the result is a concept comparison, not a one-setting experiment |
| “Make a fictional booking demo.” | Explicit fiction, coherent dates/capacities, truthful local-only outcomes; no artificial decimal precision as proof of realism |
| “Animate the benefit statement; support reduced motion.” | Optional enhancement has readable static/failure/print states and coherent accessible text; observation and progress are distinguished |
| “This paid campaign should stay out of search.” | Owner intent drives noindex; unlinked paths and robots blocking are not treated as equivalent; privacy retains access control |
| “Add a contact form and sharing preview.” | Real submission or explicit prototype boundary, validation/retry, actual deployed URLs/assets; metadata presence is not a verified consumer preview |
| “Improve our typography across languages.” | Reusable roles preserve semantic hierarchy, real strings, punctuation and available glyphs; no forced downward scale snap or universal font ban |
| “Use the million-site study to prove the design looks human.” | No authorship or preference claim is licensed by directory/HTML counts; the historical scope stays explicit |

The review identifies conflicts in the source's universal aesthetic mandates, metadata assumptions and scroll-throttling description. The v3 references contain the bounded alternatives and primary-source citations. No new application interaction, physical-device, screen-reader, conversion or preference testing is claimed.

Historical research and application validation records remain historical rather than being relabeled as v3 results.
